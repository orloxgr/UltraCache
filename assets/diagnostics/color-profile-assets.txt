UltraCache color-profile diagnostic assets
==========================================

ultracache-srgb.icc
-------------------
sRGB IEC61966-2.1 profile created by Graeme W. Gill. The profile itself states:
"Released into the public domain. No Warranty, Use at your own risk."

color-profile-self-test-cmyk.jpg
--------------------------------
Generated specifically for UltraCache as a 32x32 CMYK JPEG. It embeds the
FOGRA39L_coated ICC profile distributed by the TeX Live colorprofiles package.
That profile states that it is free of known copyright restrictions.
The JPEG pixel fixture and packaging are part of UltraCache under the plugin license.
